❖   ANCIENT LORE   ❖

QUESTIONS FROM THE MYTH

Originally a mortal priestess of extraordinary beauty dedicated to Athena, Medusa was desired by Poseidon, who pursued and desecrated her within Athena's sacred temple. Enraged by the violation of her sanctuary, Athena cursed Medusa, transforming her golden hair into venomous serpents and turning her gaze into a fatal curse that turns anyone who meets her eyes to stone.

Using Medusa's severed head safely sealed in a magic satchel (the kibisis), Perseus flew across the world on winged sandals. He rescued Princess Andromeda from the sea monster Cetus, returned to Seriphos to petrify the tyrant King Polydectes, and eventually founded the legendary ancient city of Mycenae, fulfilling prophecy and securing his place among the stars as a constellation.

King Polydectes of Seriphos lusted after Perseus's mother, Danaë, but saw the fiercely protective Perseus as an obstacle. Feigning an upcoming royal wedding to another woman, Polydectes demanded gifts of horses from everyone. Perseus, having no wealth, proudly boasted he would bring anything asked, even the Gorgon's head. The king seized upon the boast, sending Perseus on what was universally believed to be a suicide mission.
